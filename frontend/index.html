How to play

Game modes

  • Play - the classic game: guess the hidden word within the allotted tries. Type letters (or click the on-screen keyboard) and press Enter to submit a guess.
  • Assisted - same as Play, plus a live solver-hints panel next to the board that updates after every guess. See "Reading the hints panel" below.
  • Solve - for solving a puzzle from somewhere else (paper, another site, a friend), not one this app generated. Enter a guess, then manually set the color pattern it actually produced by clicking each tile to cycle miss/misplaced/exact - the solver narrows the candidate pool down from there.

Reading the hints panel (Assisted mode)

  • Candidates remaining - how many words still match everything known so far.
  • Information gained so far - a running total, in bits, of how much your guesses have narrowed the candidate pool. Higher means more narrowed down.
  • Best next guess - the single word expected to eliminate the most candidates on average, picked from the whole dictionary rather than just the remaining candidates - it's sometimes a word that can't itself be the answer, chosen purely to test letters efficiently.
  • Letters still possible - every letter that could still appear in the answer, based on guesses so far. A "×N" mark means the word contains at least N copies of that letter.
  • Candidate words - actual words still consistent with every guess made so far - this is the real remaining answer pool.
  • Elimination suggestions - extra candidate words grouped by how many still-unknown letters they contain, listed from most to least covered. These aren't necessarily good guesses to win with - they're aimed at testing unknown letters efficiently, which is a different goal from "Best next guess".

Other things worth knowing

  • Sessions persist across page reloads and resume automatically. If your browser doesn't keep that, copy the Session ID shown on the game/solve screen and paste it into "Resume a session" on the setup screen later.
  • The on-screen keyboard (Play/Assisted) can switch between QWERTY/AZERTY layouts - this only rearranges the clickable buttons; physical typing isn't affected.
  • The Theme button cycles a manual color override (Auto/Light/Dark) on top of your OS/browser's automatic setting.
